📊 Hyperliquid Sentiment & Trader Efficiency Analysis:

This repository contains an end-to-end data science project analyzing the behavior of 32 unique trading accounts on the Hyperliquid platform. The core objective was to determine how market psychology—measured by the Crypto Fear & Greed Index—impacts profitability and risk-management strategies.

🎯 Project Objective:

The goal was to move beyond raw PnL and uncover the "Alpha" behind the trades. By merging trade logs with real-time sentiment data, I identified which traders are "Snipers" (efficient with capital) and which are "Whales" (reliant on large volume).

💡 Key Findings:

•	The "Fear" Premium: Total ecosystem PnL peaked during Fear regimes (~$1.6M), suggesting that top-tier traders on Hyperliquid act as contrarian liquidity providers during market panics.
•	Efficiency vs. Aggression: During Extreme Greed, we identified a "Sniper" profile (e.g., Trader 28) who maintained top-tier profits while significantly reducing average trade size.
•	The Aggression Trap: High trade sizes do not guarantee success. Several traders (e.g., Trader 14) showed high volume churn but poor capital efficiency, losing profit to market slippage.
